1. Origins of Lorem Ipsum

        

          "Lorem Ipsum" originated in the 16th century when an unknown printer scrambled parts of Cicero's 45 BC treatise "De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum" (On the Ends of Good and Evil). The standard passage has been utilized across publishing, graphic design, and web development to showcase typographic visual hierarchy without distracting readers with intelligible semantic copy.
