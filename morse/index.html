1. Timing Proportions & The "PARIS" Standard

Standardized globally by the International Telecommunication Union (ITU-R M.1677-1), Morse code is a rhythmic binary stream where all temporal durations derive from a single unit dot (dit):

  • Dit Duration (1 Unit): The foundational time atom. At 20 WPM, 1 unit equals exactly 60 milliseconds.
  • Dah Duration (3 Units): Exactly three times the duration of a single dit.
  • Intra-Character Space (1 Unit): Silence separating dits and dahs within the same character.
  • Inter-Character Space (3 Units): Silence separating consecutive letters.
  • Word Space (7 Units): Silence separating distinct words (represented by forward slash / in raw text).

Transmission speed is calibrated against the international test word "PARIS ", which contains exactly 50 elemental units: Unit Duration (ms) = 1200 / WPM.
