        Glassmorphism is a UI design language characterized by translucent multi-layered surfaces, frosted-glass backdrop filters, subtle specular highlights, and vibrant neon accents:
      


      

        

          1. Hardware Acceleration
          CSS backdrop-filter operates directly on the compositor layer in modern GPU pipelines, maintaining 60+ FPS scrolling.
        

        

          2. WebKit Prefixing
          Generates both standard backdrop-filter and vendor-prefixed -webkit-backdrop-filter for full Safari iOS/macOS compatibility.
        

        

          3. Light Bleed Edges
          A semi-transparent 1px border simulates specular light refraction at physical glass boundaries.
