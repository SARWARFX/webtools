          When social platforms like Twitter/X, Facebook, LinkedIn, Discord, or Slack process a shared URL, their link unfurlers crawl the HTML document's `` to construct visual rich snippets:
        

        
    
          
  • og:title: The primary title of your object. Keep under 60 characters to prevent truncation across mobile viewports.
    • 
          
  • og:description: A 1-2 sentence snippet of the content. Ideal range is 55–160 characters.
    • 
          
  • og:image: Absolute URL to an image asset. Recommended aspect ratio is 1.91:1 (1200×630 pixels) with maximum file size under 5MB.
    • 
          
  • og:url: The canonical URL for your page. Consolidates likes, reactions, and share telemetry across HTTP/HTTPS and query param variations.
    • 
          
  • twitter:card: Card presentation format: summary_large_image (full-bleed 1200×630 banner) or summary (square thumbnail).
