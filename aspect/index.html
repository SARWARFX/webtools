Euclidean Greatest Common Divisor

To express the aspect ratio of two pixel dimensions (such as 1920 and 1080) in lowest integer terms, the Euclidean algorithm determines the Greatest Common Divisor (GCD). By iteratively replacing the larger number by the remainder of dividing the larger by the smaller: GCD(1920, 1080) = 120 Dividing both numbers by 120 yields the canonical proportion 16:9.
