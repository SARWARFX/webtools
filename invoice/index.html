KNOWLEDGE_BASE // FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Invoice Generation & Billing FAQs

Can I generate and save PDF invoices without an account?

Yes. There are no logins, passwords, paywalls, or subscriptions. Clicking "Download PDF" leverages native browser vector rendering to generate clean, unwatermarked invoices instantly.

Will my invoice data disappear if I close or refresh the tab?

No. Every keystroke, currency choice, line item, and uploaded logo is automatically saved to your browser's private local storage. When you return to the page, your invoice is restored exactly as you left it.

Which currencies and tax structures are supported?

You can issue invoices in USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), JPY (¥), CAD (CA$), AUD (AU$), INR (₹), or CHF. The calculation engine accommodates custom sales tax, VAT, GST, and percentage discounts.
