Why Use a Burner Mailbox?

In modern web workflows, software engineers, security auditors, and researchers routinely sign up for evaluation services, test multi-tenant account workflows, or download whitepapers. Exposing personal or corporate email addresses invariably leads to vendor newsletters, telemetry tracking, and inclusion in marketing lead broker databases.

CyberTools Disposable Inbox provides instant, zero-login email addresses powered by open public mail gateways. You can receive OTP verification codes, activate accounts, and abandon the mailbox with zero trace of personal identity.

Client-Side Architecture & Sound Alerts

The Burner Mailbox operates completely client-side in your browser. Upon generating an address, the tab begins continuous 5-second polling of the public message queue.

When a new transmission arrives, the tool invokes the native browser Web Audio API to synthesize a dual-frequency audio chime alert (880 Hz → 1760 Hz) directly through your device speakers, ensuring you never miss a time-sensitive verification code while multitasking.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do disposable addresses remain active?

Addresses remain active as long as you keep your browser tab open or retain the email credentials. Inboxes are purged by public mail relays after several hours of inactivity.

Can I send emails from this burner mailbox?

No. To prevent abuse and spam distribution, the 1secmail public protocol is strictly inbound-only. It is engineered solely for receiving verification tokens, confirmations, and newsletters.

Is my real IP address linked to the mailbox?

No. Mail is routed through decentralized edge relays. No account registration, cookies, or persistent user tracking identifiers are tied to your browsing session.

Can I mute the audio chime notification?

Yes. Click the 'CHIME: ON/OFF' toggle button in the active address bar to silence Web Audio synthesizer alerts at any time.
