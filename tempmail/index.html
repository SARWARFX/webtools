          IN-DEPTH GUIDE // DUAL-GATEWAY BURNER INBOX & GMAIL REPUTATION ROUTING
        

        

          Understanding high-reputation disposable domains, anti-suppression architecture, sandboxed payload execution, and zero-trust identity isolation.
        

      


      

        

          
Node 01: Mail.tm Network (Anti-Suppression)

          

            Major mail providers such as Google Gmail, Microsoft Outlook, and corporate anti-spam gateways (Proofpoint, Mimecast) maintain dynamic blacklists of well-known disposable email domains. When signing up for sensitive services or developer platforms, messages sent to legacy domains are frequently silently dropped or quarantined.
          

          

            Mail.tm provisions temporary inboxes across high-reputation domains with authenticated SPF, DKIM, and DMARC DNS records. This eliminates deliverability suppression and ensures instant receipt of one-time verification tokens and confirmation hyperlinks.
          

        


        

          
Node 02: 1secmail Network (Stateless Fallback)

          

            As a decentralized redundancy layer, 1secmail operates a lightweight, stateless mail server. If the primary gateway experiences maintenance or edge rate-limits, you can switch to Node 02 with a single click.
          

          

            The Burner Mail Radar runs 100% in-browser. All authentication tokens and state reside exclusively in volatile memory and are cleared when the tab is closed or a new address is generated.
          

        

      


      

        
Security & Sandboxed Isolation

        

          Incoming email messages may contain tracking pixels, hostile scripts, or unescaped HTML. CyberTools renders all received message payloads inside an isolated, sandboxed <iframe sandbox="allow-same-origin allow-popups"> element. This isolates the parent HUD application from hostile script injection while allowing you to safely read messages and inspect links.
        


        

          

            
How long do disposable addresses remain active?

            

              Addresses remain active as long as you keep your browser tab open or retain the email credentials. Inboxes are purged by public mail relays after several hours of inactivity.
            

          

          

            
Can I send emails from this burner mailbox?

            

              No. To prevent abuse and spam distribution, both gateways are strictly inbound-only. They are engineered solely for receiving verification tokens, confirmations, and newsletters.
            

          

          

            
Is my real IP address linked to the mailbox?

            

              No. Mail is routed through decentralized edge relays. No account registration, cookies, or persistent user tracking identifiers are tied to your browsing session.
            

          

          

            
How does the Web Audio chime work?

            

              The tool uses the browser Web Audio API to synthesize a dual-frequency audio tone (880 Hz to 1760 Hz) directly in hardware when a new transmission is detected during radar sweeps.
